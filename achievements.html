Major Milestones

2023

TeenX Founded

TeenX begins with a vision to empower young people as trafficking prevention agents.

First 1,000 Youth Envoys Trained

Reached milestone of training 1,000+ community-based Youth Envoys across all 64 districts of Bangladesh.
