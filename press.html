February 2026

Government Announces Partnership with TeenX for National Youth Protection Strategy

The Government of Bangladesh has formally partnered with TeenX to integrate the Youth-Led Prevention Model into the National Child Protection Strategy. This marks a significant milestone in scaling youth-based trafficking prevention across all government institutions and represents government validation of our community-based approach.

Recent Announcements

March 2026

TeenX 2025 Impact Report Released: 240 Cases Prevented, Zero Re-trafficking

TeenX releases comprehensive 2025 Annual Report documenting unprecedented impact: 240 trafficking cases prevented or resolved, 500,000+ families reached through awareness campaigns, 99% survivor survival rate, and 0% re-trafficking rate among economically supported survivors.

Read →
January 2025

#Protected Campaign Goes Nationwide: Reaching 5 Million Bangladeshis

TeenX launches #Protected nationwide awareness campaign focusing on trafficking prevention, survivor support, and community mobilization. Campaign operates across digital platforms, radio, SMS, school assemblies, and community events across Bangladesh.

Read →
August 2024

TeenX Reaches Historic Milestone: 1,000 Youth Envoys Trained Across All 64 Districts

TeenX announces completion of Youth Protection Network expansion: 1,000+ trained youth protection coordinators now operational in every district of Bangladesh. Network has prevented 150+ documented trafficking cases in 2024 with rapid emergency response capabilities.

Read →
December 2023

TeenX Bangladesh Officially Launched: New Youth-Led Anti-Trafficking Organization

TeenX Bangladesh officially launches as the first youth-led national anti-trafficking organization. The organization positions teenagers as agents of community protection rather than passive victims of trafficking.
